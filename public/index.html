      About ResumeFit AI

      ResumeFit AI is a modern, AI-powered resume analyzer and job matching intelligence platform. It analyzes candidate resumes against job descriptions to provide deep, transparent insights into role readiness and skill compatibility.

      Purpose

      Job seekers frequently apply without knowing how well their resume aligns with specific hiring requirements or automated Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS). ResumeFit AI bridges this gap by comparing your resume directly with real job descriptions, highlighting critical gaps, and recommending concrete adjustments to maximize your interview chances.

      Key Features

      • Intelligent Resume Analysis: Parses PDF and Word (.docx) resumes to extract professional background, technical competencies, and project history.
      • Targeted Job Matching: Evaluates alignment against role responsibilities, required qualifications, and industry keywords.
      • Skill Gap Identification: Identifies matched competencies alongside missing required and preferred skills.
      • Transparent Score Breakdown: Computes an explainable match score using weighted categories: Skills (60%), Experience (25%), and Projects (15%).
      • Actionable Recommendations: Delivers personalized suggestions and keyword advice for targeted resume tuning.
      • Authenticated History & Guest Mode: Enjoy complete freedom with instant Guest Mode or sign in to permanently save, revisit, and manage your analysis history.
      ⚠️

      AI Accuracy Notice: AI-generated results, match scores, and recommendations are automated estimates intended for guidance only. They do not guarantee job offers or interview selection. Please review all insights and verify suggestions against your genuine background.

      Help & Support

      How to Use ResumeFit AI

      1. 1
        Open ResumeFit AI in Guest Mode or log in to your account.

        You can start immediately as a guest without creating an account, or log in to save your reports.

      2. 2
        Upload your resume in a supported format.

        Drag and drop or browse for your resume in PDF (.pdf) or Word (.docx) format (up to 5 MB).

      3. 3
        Paste the complete job description.

        Paste the full job post text, including responsibilities, requirements, and preferred qualifications (minimum 50 characters).

      4. 4
        Enter the job title and company name if desired.

        These optional fields help contextualize your analysis report and organize saved entries.

      5. 5
        Click Analyze Resume.

        Our pipeline parses your resume text and evaluates it using Gemini AI against the job description.

      6. 6
        Review the overall match score.

        See your transparent 0–100 match score with a visual score ring and category score bars.

      7. 7
        Examine matched skills and missing required or preferred skills.

        Identify which competencies you already meet and what key technical or soft skills are absent.

      8. 8
        Review experience, projects, keywords, and improvement recommendations.

        Read detailed breakdowns and tailored suggestions to refine your resume bullet points.

      9. 9
        Log in or create an account if you want your analyses saved in your personal history.

        Registered users can reopen previous reports anytime and manage their analysis history.

      👤 Guest Mode Privacy

      Guest analysis results are temporary and kept in memory only for your current session. Guest history is never saved to our database.

      🔒 Authenticated History

      Logged-in users can safely revisit, review, and delete their saved analyses anytime through the My History section.
